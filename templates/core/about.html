About ttvdrops.lovinator.space

ttvdrops.lovinator.space tracks limited-time free rewards (drops) across Twitch, Kick, and Chzzk. Streaming platforms often give away free in-game items, channel perks, and other rewards to viewers who meet certain conditions, like watching a streamer play a game for a set amount of time.

These offers are only available for a limited window and can be scattered across different platforms, making them easy to miss.

ttvdrops.lovinator.space monitors these campaigns, archives their data for historical reference, and lets you subscribe to RSS/Atom feeds so you never miss a new offer.

How Drops Work

Twitch Drops are organized into Drops Campaigns: collections of rewards distributed during a specified time period. Each campaign has a start and end date and is tied to a specific game category. Within a campaign, you can have multiple Drops, each with its own conditions (called Drop Rules, also referred to as milestones or requirements) that viewers must meet to earn a reward.

There are two types of drop requirements:

  • Time-Based Drops (Watch Time): Viewers earn rewards by watching streams of the game for a set amount of time, such as 30 or 60 minutes. Watch time accumulates across all channels streaming the game, so you can hop between streamers.
  • Subscription-Based Drops (Gifts & Subs): Viewers earn rewards by subscribing to or gifting subscriptions on channels where Drops are enabled. Each subscription counts toward the requirement, accumulating across all participating channels.

When a viewer meets a Drop's requirement, a Reward (also called a Benefit) is granted: typically an in-game item or beta-access key. To claim their rewards, viewers must first link their Twitch account with the game's identity service via an Account Linking page, then visit their Drops Inventory to claim.

Campaigns pass through several states before and after they run:

  • Upcoming - The campaign is scheduled but hasn't started yet.
  • Live - The campaign is active and Drops can be earned.
  • Expired - The campaign has ended. Once expired, rewards that were claimed remain in a viewer's inventory for 6 months.

A campaign can include multiple Drops that run simultaneously, for example, one reward after 30 minutes of watching and another after 60 minutes. All time-based Drops accrue watch time at the same time, so you can earn toward all of them at once. Campaigns can run for up to 28 days, and only one category-wide campaign can run for a given game at a time.

Features

  • Multi-platform: Twitch, Kick, and Chzzk
  • RSS/Atom feeds: Subscribe to campaigns, games, organizations, and rewards
  • Historical archive: Past campaigns remain searchable and downloadable
  • Search and browse: Find campaigns by game, organization, channel, or reward
  • Open data: All data is freely available under CC0
